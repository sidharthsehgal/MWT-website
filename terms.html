5. Parent supervision and safety

MyWunderTale stories are intended for listening and imagination, not as instructions to act out story events. A parent or guardian should supervise app use and decide whether a story, theme, character action, or activity mentioned in a story is appropriate for each child. Children should not attempt unsafe, risky, unlawful, or unsupervised actions because they heard them in a story.

The sleep timer and autoplay features let stories continue after a parent leaves the room. These are convenience features, not supervision. You remain responsible for the device, the volume level, the use of headphones, and the child's surroundings.

6. Device, hearing, and sleep safety

  • Hearing safety: Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially when a child uses headphones or earbuds. Prolonged listening at high volume can damage hearing.
  • Device temperature and charging: Narration is generated on the device while a story plays. This is intensive processing that can warm the device and use battery. Do not cover a playing device with pillows, blankets, or bedding, and do not leave a device charging in a child's bed.
  • Safe sleep environment: Keep devices, cables, and chargers out of cribs and bassinets and away from infants and toddlers. Follow the safe-sleep guidance of your pediatrician or local health authority.
  • Distracted situations: Do not interact with the app while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention.
  • Non-medical disclaimer: MyWunderTale is not a sleep aid, sleep training programme, or medical or therapeutic product, and it is not a baby monitor or an emergency or safety service.

MyWunderTale creates personalised audio stories by inserting your child's name and pronouns, and optionally how a parent or caregiver is addressed, into fictional story templates. The stories are entertainment only. They are not biographical, educational, psychological, or factual accounts of your child or family.

Story characters share your child's name but are fictional. Story events, traits, actions, and portrayals are generated from templates and are not intended to represent your child's real personality, abilities, or experiences. Attain AI Labs LLC is not responsible for any dissatisfaction with how a character based on your child's name is depicted in a story.

Because stories use your child's name, a child may feel that story events happened to them or reflect on them personally. Some stories include mild suspense, mistakes, disappointment, fear, or sadness that is resolved within the story, as is common in children's literature. Age ranges shown in the app are general guidance only and are not a rating or certification by any board or authority. We recommend that a parent or guardian listens to a story first, or listens alongside a young child the first time a story is played. If a child is upset or frightened by a story, stop playback, talk with your child, and hide the story from the library. Attain AI Labs LLC is not responsible for a child's emotional reaction to story content.

By entering a child's name into the app, you consent to that name being used to personalise story content in this way. If you are not comfortable with a particular story's portrayal, you can hide that story from the library.

If you do not enter a child profile, some stories may use default placeholder names. These names are fictional template defaults. They are not inferred from your device, account, contacts, location, microphone, photos, online sources, or any other personal data source. Any match to a real person is coincidental.
