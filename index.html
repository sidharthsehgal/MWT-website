A personalized kids story app for calm bedtime routines

MyWunderTale is a free personalized kids story app for iPhone, iPad and Android that reads original audio stories aloud with your children as the stars. Add your kids' names once, pick favorite themes, and every tale casts them as the hero — from space adventures to toothbrush parades before sleep.

Designed as a screen-free audio alternative, stories are narrated by natural on-device voices with zero streaming, no ads, and no internet required. Perfect for peaceful bedtimes, road trips, and flights.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is MyWunderTale?

MyWunderTale is a free personalized kids story app for iPhone, iPad and Android that reads original audio stories aloud with your child's name woven into every story. It is free to download and contains no ads.

How does MyWunderTale personalize stories?

You add your child's first name, age and pronouns, and optionally what they call you, like Mom or Dad. The app weaves those details into each story and its title, then reads it aloud. Siblings can share a story as co-stars.

Does MyWunderTale work offline?

Yes. The app does not require Internet connectivity, so stories play with no Wi-Fi or cellular data, which makes it good for flights, road trips and places with poor signal.

Is my child's information private?

Yes. Names, ages and profile details stay on your device. The app does not transmit or share any data. Feedback data is only sent if a grown-up chooses to submit feedback.

Is MyWunderTale free? Does it have ads?

MyWunderTale is free to download and has no ads.

What ages is MyWunderTale for?

The stories are written for children roughly 3 to 12 years old, and each story shows its suggested age range.

Can I make sure the narrator says my child's name correctly?

Yes. You can pick how the narrator pronounces each name, preview it out loud, and the story then uses exactly that pronunciation. Please share feedback if the names are not read correctly.
